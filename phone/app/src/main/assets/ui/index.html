    How it works

    Each phone plays a short chirp 23 times a second and listens for both. Sound moves faster through hot air, so when warm air crosses the line between them, both chirps arrive a little early.

    Tap Not a flame when something else crosses the line, like a person, a hand or a fan, and again when it stops. Saved tests then show what the alarm should ignore.

    Adding the two directions together cancels moving air (it speeds one way up as much as it slows the other) and the two phones' clocks. What is left is heat.

    The other phone's address

    It's shown on the other phone's screen, under this phone's name. Use it if the phones don't find each other by themselves.

    Distance between the phones

    Measure from speaker to speaker with a tape, in cm. The speed of sound is then worked out over the real distance, instead of taking the air to be 20 °C at the start.
